About Me

My Journey into Data Science

Hi, I'm Ghson Alotibi, a data-driven problem solver based in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. I hold an MBA and a Master of Science in Data Science from Willamette University (2025, GPA 3.71), and bring 6+ years of experience across Saudi Arabia and the United States.

My passion for data began in the dental clinic, where I saw firsthand how operational insights could improve systems and outcomes. That fueled my interest in analytics, financial modeling, and machine learning.

My StrokeSense AI stroke-detection platform was recognized by the Saudi Ministry of Health and SDAIA in October 2025. I've also built a market manipulation detection system and an Opportunity Index for Oregon counties, working at the intersection of technology, impact, and strategy.

I'm looking for roles in strategy, data science, and analytics where I can turn data into decisions for high-impact initiatives.
